# Flightglass Guide surface

Mode: Operate

Approved comp: `.impeccable/mocks/guide-concept-b-question-ladder.png`

Owner approval is delegated for the autonomous 2026-08-07 build. Concept B is
selected because its expanding question ladder makes hierarchy and progressive
depth legible without turning the surface into a generic card dashboard.

## Direction contract

Flightglass Guide is a dark field instrument organized as one vertical chain of
questions and evidence. A compact Trajectory Aperture establishes the product
world; the page then behaves like a measured notebook: full-width question rows
branch into indented evidence, deterministic geometry and exact tables. Violet
lines express hierarchy, cyan separates directional comparison, and ember marks
only the live resolving point or primary action. The composition is dense but
not cramped, with separators and spacing doing most of the grouping. There is
no chat metaphor, assistant avatar, giant hero, FAQ grid or decorative glass.
On desktop, the same ladder becomes a narrow topic rail beside the evidence
lens; it does not become a dashboard. Motion is a single causal trace resolving
from an already-readable state. The generated club-and-ball picture in the comp
is not literal: measurable geometry must be crisp deterministic SVG generated
from engine values. The comp's sample numbers and causal claims are illustrative
layout content only and must be replaced with authoritative Guide outputs.

## Component grammar

- Corners: 12-16 px plates; small status labels may be pills.
- Lines: 1 px canonical separators; a 2 px causal trace is allowed only inside
  deterministic diagrams.
- Elevation: top-lit plate or one soft offset shadow, never border plus shadow.
- Type: established Flightglass display for answer conclusions, UI sans for
  questions/body, mono/tabular only for measurements and provenance.
- Controls: full-width question rows and compact intent buttons, all at least
  44 px. Active state uses violet structure; ember is reserved for the primary
  action/live point.

another. At 1440x900, topic ladder and evidence lens may coexist. Reading order
is identical, no horizontal scroll is required, and tables can scroll inside a
