Frequently asked questions

How do I restore Flightglass Pro?
  1. Make sure the device uses the same Apple ID or Google account that made the purchase.
  2. Connect to the internet.
  3. Open Flightglass Pro and choose Restore Purchases. Restore is also available from Home.
  4. Wait for the confirmation, then return to the action that opened Pro.

If restore still fails, restart the app and try once more. Then email support with your platform and approximate purchase date. Do not send your store password.

I purchased Pro, but the app is still locked

First use Restore Purchases. Check that the purchase is active in your App Store or Google Play subscriptions and that the device is online.

If the store shows an active purchase but Flightglass remains locked, contact support. Include the store platform, product type (Monthly, Annual or legacy Lifetime), device model and the time the restore was attempted.

How do I cancel a subscription or request a refund?

Subscriptions are billed and managed by Apple or Google, not directly by Flightglass. Cancel in your device's App Store or Google Play subscription settings. Refund decisions are also handled by the store where the purchase was made.

Deleting Flightglass does not cancel an active subscription.

Does Mechanics Lab require landscape?

No. Mechanics Lab adapts to portrait and landscape. Controls, Cause Trace and the six flight outcomes reflow without a forced-rotate screen.

Use either orientation. A wider view places more of the causal instrument side by side; portrait keeps the same controls and facts in a vertical flow.

Where is my saved data?

Flightglass v1 has no account or cloud sync. Onboarding progress, saved model context, free-use counters and local preferences stay on the device in app storage.

Clearing website/app data or removing the app can erase local progress. Store purchases remain with Apple or Google and can be restored.

Is Flightglass measuring or diagnosing my golf swing?

No. Flightglass is an educational deterministic model. It does not connect to a launch monitor, capture video, measure a real shot, diagnose technique or prescribe a personalised fitting.

Mechanics Lab models four Impact Inputs or four Arc Inputs at a time. Range replays modelled setups, and Guide labels bounded estimates and unsupported real-world questions instead of inventing precision.

Can I type my own question into Flightglass Guide?

Not in v1. Guide uses predefined questions and guided buttons so every answer can stay within the shipping model's evidence. There is no free-text or generative-AI request path.

How do I request access to or deletion of my data?

Flightglass has no user account and does not collect your name, email, swing video or free-text content. Purchases are processed by Apple or Google and subscription status is managed through RevenueCat using an anonymous app-user identifier.

Email sivertskotvold@gmail.com with “Flightglass privacy request” in the subject. State your platform and whether you are requesting access or deletion. We may need a non-secret purchase or app identifier to locate the anonymous record.

Apple and Google retain their own transaction records under their policies. See the Privacy Policy for details.

Useful details to include

Help us reproduce the issue: device model, operating-system version, Flightglass screen, the control you used, expected result, actual result, and whether the issue repeats after restarting. A screenshot is useful when it contains no private store information.
