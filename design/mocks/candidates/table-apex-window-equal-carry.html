Same carry, different ceiling — the apex window

Hold spin loft at 34° and club speed at 95 mph, then trade attack angle for delivered loft one degree for one degree. Along that diagonal the ball does not care which of the two you moved: every row carries exactly 185.81 yd, lands at the same 50.97°, spins the same 7413 rpm and leaves the face at the same 128.64 mph.

One number moves, and only one. Apex climbs from 27.8 yd to 41.8 yd, a 14 yd window across the four deliveries. Steeper attack with less loft buys a flat, boring ceiling; shallower attack with more loft buys a high one. Same distance, same landing angle, different flight to get there. That window is the whole choice.

Apex is tagged ESTIMATE: it is engine output, not a measured ball flight. The orange rule beside each apex figure is that same number drawn to scale, measured linearly from 0 yd on one shared 0–45 yd axis. It is not zoomed and it does not exaggerate. The figure is the truth; the rule is only a second reading of it.

Typical marks a delivery an ordinary player actually produces. Extreme marks one that is correctly computed and inside the engine domain but outside the normal player window: 24° / −10° is a very steep punch, and 40° / +6° cannot be produced off the ground with a mid-iron at all. Both are bounds on the window, not recommendations.

So read the 14 yd figure as the full width of the window, not as a shot menu. Between the two typical deliveries on their own, 28° and 34°, apex moves 5.1 yd, from 30.7 yd to 35.8 yd. That is the part of the window an ordinary player can actually choose between.

Where the equal-carry claim stops

This diagonal only holds carry constant above a knee at 22.83° delivered loft (−11.17° attack). Below it, launch angle drops under 10°, carry launch efficiency falls below 1, and carry starts falling with it.

The originally specified grid opened at 22° delivered loft, which is under that knee. Re-derived, that row carries 180.96 yd, which is 4.84 yd short of the other three, so a single shared carry figure would have been false. The table above therefore opens at 24°, the smallest change that makes the premise true; nothing else in the grid moved.
