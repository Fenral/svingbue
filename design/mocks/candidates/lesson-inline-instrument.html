01

Model term

Spin loft is the whole story.

The instrument has re-framed. It is no longer drawing where the ball goes. It is drawing the geometry that decides the spin: the gap between where the club is travelling and where the face is pointing.

spin loft = dynamic loft − attack angle

Right now the face delivers of dynamic loft while the head travels at of attack. That is — of spin loft, and it is the only geometric quantity the spin number depends on.

Hitting down does not create spin in the ground. It widens the gap, and the gap is what the face converts into revolutions while the ball is still on it. Both rails feed the same term, which is why they can cancel each other exactly.

03

Engine limit

One ceiling. No floor.

Two bars now: what the interface shows you, and what the model is actually holding. Push spin loft up and they part company at 9,000 rpm. Push it down and they never part at all.

The cap is an interface decision, not physics. Below it, display and model are the same number. Above it, only the display stops, which is why the sensitivity figures on the rails keep moving when the big numeral does not.

There is nothing equivalent at the bottom. Spin is generated by the glancing blow, so as spin loft goes toward zero the spin goes with it, all the way down. A square strike with no spin loft imparts no backspin at all.

Limits: academy-backspin-model.js → BACKSPIN_LIMITS.max = 9000, LOW_SPIN_NOTE_RPM = 1500. Copy: SHEETS.displayCeiling, SHEETS.noFloor.

What the simulator does not know.
